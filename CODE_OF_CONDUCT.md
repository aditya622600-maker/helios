# Team conduct

Helios is a time-constrained collaborative engineering project. Team members agree to:

- communicate blockers early and concretely;
- critique code and evidence, not people;
- preserve other members' work;
- document assumptions and limitations;
- avoid overstating model accuracy or data quality;
- review urgent pull requests promptly;
- keep credentials and private data secure;
- credit datasets, research and team contributions.

Escalate unresolved scope or contract disagreements to the team lead. During the feature-freeze period, correctness and integration take priority over new features.
